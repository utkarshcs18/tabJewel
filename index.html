About Tab Jewellers

Where tradition meets
refined craftsmanship

For generations, Tab Jewellers has been part of Kolhapur's celebrations — weddings, festivals, milestones, and quiet everyday moments that still deserve something beautiful. We are not only a jewellery store; we are craftspeople, advisors, and neighbours who understand what a single piece can mean to a family.

Our showroom in Shahupuri brings together the warmth of a family jeweller and the standards of a modern house: curated designs, certified materials, and service that respects your time and your budget. Whether you are planning a full bridal trousseau, choosing a gift for someone you love, or upgrading a piece you wear every day, we help you find jewellery that feels right — in weight, in style, and in spirit.

Every ornament that leaves our counter is checked for finish, symmetry, and comfort. We work with BIS-hallmarked gold and certified diamonds, and we explain purity, weight, and making charges before you decide — so you always know exactly what you are buying. That transparency has earned us the trust of customers who return not just for purchases, but for repairs, resizing, exchanges, and advice across years.

From temple-inspired bridal sets and antique-finish necklaces to sleek diamond studs and lightweight pendants, our collections reflect Kolhapur's love for gold while staying current with how people dress today. And when you want something truly your own, our team works with you on custom designs — sketches, stone selection, trials, and final delivery — so the piece you wear carries your story, not just our catalogue.

“Jewellery should outlive the occasion — and the trust behind it should outlive us.” — Tab Jewellers, Kolhapur

Why families choose us

  • ◆

    Hallmarked Purity

    Every gold piece is BIS-hallmarked; diamonds are certified. We welcome questions about karat, weight, and certification — because confidence in purity is the foundation of every purchase.

  • ◆

    Custom Designs

    Share a reference, a family heirloom, or a rough idea — our artisans and designers help you build bespoke bridal sets, rings, pendants, and gift pieces tailored to your taste and occasion.

  • ◆

    Trusted Legacy

    Parents who shopped with us bring their children; brides recommend us to friends. That continuity is built on honest dealings, consistent quality, and respect for every customer who walks in.

  • ◆

    Fair Pricing

    Live gold rates, clear making charges, and detailed bills — no last-minute surprises. We believe luxury should feel clear, not confusing, whether you are buying your first chain or your wedding set.

  • ◆

    Bridal Expertise

    From full trousseau planning to matching necklaces, bangles, and earrings, we guide brides and families through selections that photograph beautifully and feel comfortable through long ceremonies.

  • ◆

    After-Sales Care

    Cleaning, minor repairs, resizing, and honest guidance on care at home — we stand behind what we sell and help your jewellery stay as meaningful as the day you received it.
